Official Request · Reopening of Cas di Cultura
CAS DI CULTURA
MAYBELLINE ARENDS-CROES

A call to name the building in her honor

To:

The Minister of General Affairs, Culture, Environment and Nature

Prime Minister Mr. Mike Eman

Aruba

Subject: Request to name Cas di Cultura in honor of Mrs. Aura Maybelline Arends-Croes

Dear Minister,

With this letter we request that you, in the context of the renovation and renewal of Cas di Cultura, consider naming the renewed national culture and theater building after Mrs. Aura Maybelline Arends-Croes. In our view, such a naming would constitute a fitting and lasting recognition of her exceptional contribution to music, art, culture, education, the Papiamento language, and Aruba's national identity.

Since its founding in 1958, Cas di Cultura has fulfilled a central role in Aruba's cultural life. Now that the building is undergoing a thorough renovation and entering a new phase in its history, we consider this a particularly fitting moment not only to invest in the theater's physical future, but also to link the building's name to a person whose life's work is closely interwoven with Aruba's cultural development.

In our conviction, Mrs. Aura Maybelline Arends-Croes belongs to this special category of culture bearers. Her contribution is distinguished in particular along four interconnected lines: education, cultural and musical projects, national identity, and public recognition.

Education and formation

After completing her music education at the Music Conservatory in Hilversum, she returned to Aruba in 1968. She subsequently worked for forty years as a music teacher, including at Colegio Arubano and the former Academia Pedagogico Arubano, later the Instituto Pedagogico Arubano. In doing so, she not only musically shaped generations of young people but also trained future teachers, exercising a lasting influence on the place of music and art within Aruban education.

Cultural and musical projects

Her contribution extends considerably beyond formal education, however. She founded and led several choirs and vocal groups, including the youth choir Tutti Frutti, Sunrise, and the vocal groups Melange and Young Voices. She also guided children, young people, soloists, artists, and musicians over many years. Under her musical direction, numerous concerts, festivals, musicals, television productions, national celebrations, and cultural events came about, both on Aruba and abroad. Over the years, Cas di Cultura also served as an important stage for her work, her students, and her cultural and musical projects.

National identity and historical significance

Of particular national significance is her contribution to a key moment in Aruba's constitutional and identity-related development. Mrs. Arends-Croes was a member of the commission that advised the government in the process leading to the official adoption of Aruba's national anthem. During the official proclamation of the Aruban flag and national anthem on March 18, 1976, Aruba Dushi Tera was performed officially for the first time by the young voices of her youth choir Tutti Frutti, under her direction. Her work has thereby secured a lasting place in Aruba's national memory and in the broader process of awareness that contributed to Aruba's development as an autonomous country within the Kingdom of the Netherlands.

Language, heritage, and artistic transmission

Mrs. Arends-Croes also made an important contribution as a composer, conductor, musical accompanist, and producer. She worked for many years to promote the use of Papiamento in music and song. She also composed, translated, and adapted songs and contributed to numerous cultural and musical projects and song collections. Her work thus represents not only artistic value but also contributes to the preservation and transmission of Aruba's language and cultural heritage.

Public recognition

The societal significance of her work is also evident from the various honors she received during her career. She was appointed Knight in the Order of Orange-Nassau and received the first Aruba-UNESCO Music Award for her contribution to a culture of peace through music. She also received the Tapushi Literario for her contribution to the promotion of Papiamento and was honored with a cultural award from the Prince Bernhard Cultuurfonds Caribbean Region.

What distinguishes her life's work, in our view, is not solely the length of her career or the number of cultural and musical projects to which she contributed. Her significance lies largely in the generations she has shaped. From among her pupils and students have emerged musicians, conductors, composers, music teachers, and other culture bearers who have, in turn, further developed Aruba's cultural life. Her influence has therefore not been limited to her own work but lives on in Aruba's cultural life.

Precisely because her life's work connects education, the performing arts, language, heritage, and national identity, we consider naming the renewed culture and theater building after Mrs. Aura Maybelline Arends-Croes a particularly fitting form of recognition. Cas di Cultura is, above all, the place where these various dimensions of culture come together and can be passed on to new generations.

Such a naming would, moreover, convey a meaningful message to future generations: that a country is built not solely through political and economic developments, but also by those who preserve, develop, and pass on its language, music, art, stories, and cultural identity.

We therefore respectfully request that you consider the proposal to give the renewed Cas di Cultura the name:

"Cas di Cultura Maybelline Arends-Croes"

or to explicitly include this naming in the formal decision-making process regarding the reopening, renovation, and future positioning of the culture and theater building.

We trust that this proposal will be assessed on its cultural, historical, and societal merits. We are, of course, willing to further elaborate on this request and to make relevant historical documentation available in support, should you wish.

In support of this request, a brief explanatory note and a statement of support are enclosed as appendices.

Sincerely,

Initiative submitted by Igor Arends and the Aruban community supporting this request.

Appendix I · Explanatory note on the nomination

Her career summarized in five key points

Music education

Forty years active in education, involved in the musical formation of generations of pupils and future teachers.

Cultural development

Founder and conductor of several choirs and vocal groups, involved in countless concerts, festivals, musicals, and cultural productions.

National identity

Involved in the process surrounding the national anthem and conductor of Tutti Frutti at its official performance in 1976.

Language and heritage

Promotion of Papiamento through compositions, translations, adaptations, song collections, and music productions.

Lasting impact

Training and guidance of generations who later became active themselves in music, education, and culture in Aruba.

Cas di Cultura Maybelline Arends-Croes would stand for:

  • The value of art and music education
  • The transmission of culture from generation to generation
  • The place of Papiamento within music and art
  • The contribution of women to Aruba's development
  • The role of artists and teachers in shaping national identity
  • The importance of voluntary cultural commitment to the community
